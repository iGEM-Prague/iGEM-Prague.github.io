## Who We Are

We are BohemiaBio, a student team that brings together different fields of study and universities. Our project CrocoVoyage, which we are preparing for the iGEM 2027 competition, focuses on developing cyanobacteria of the genus *Chroococcidiopsis* as organisms for synthetic biology and future space biotechnology. Long-term space missions will need technologies that make it possible to produce the necessary substances on site and to reduce dependence on supplies from Earth. *Chroococcidiopsis* interests us because of its ability to photosynthesize and the resistance of some strains to radiation, desiccation and other harsh conditions. However, its use is still limited by the lack of established methods for cultivation and genetic modification.

Our main goal is to develop and validate protocols that make working with this organism easier. We want to optimize cultivation conditions and methods of transformation and genetic modification. We will also focus on characterizing genetic parts, especially promoters, terminators and origins of replication. These parts will allow us to control the expression of inserted genes and to maintain genetic constructs. We want to make the resulting protocols and results available to other researchers.

Our long-term ambition is to use the tools we develop to engineer cyanobacteria for specific applications, such as the production of vitamins and pharmaceuticals. In this way, we want to lay the foundations for a wider use of *Chroococcidiopsis* in space biotechnology and to bring this topic closer to the public.

We also want to help build a synthetic biology community at the participating universities and to support future generations of Czech students in taking part in the iGEM competition.

## The iGEM Competition

iGEM is an international student competition in synthetic biology, originally from MIT. Its culmination, the Grand Jamboree, takes place every year in Paris. Teams from all over the world design solutions to various problems using synthetic biology and test them experimentally.

Synthetic biology combines biology with engineering and makes it possible to modify organisms so that they perform desired functions. Thanks to iGEM, we can experience the whole research process, from the first idea through laboratory work to presenting the results. We also collaborate with experts and consider the societal benefits and safety of the project, as well as ways to communicate science. You can find more about the iGEM competition at [igem.org](https://igem.org).
